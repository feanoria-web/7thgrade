Key Phrases

🎯 Making Invitations

  • Would you like...?
  • How about...?
  • Let's...

✅ Accepting Invitations

  • That sounds great!
  • That sounds fun!
  • I'd love to!
  • Sure!

❌ Refusing Invitations

  • I'm sorry, but I can't.
  • I'd love to, but...

💬 Giving Reasons

  • Because...
  • I must...
  • I have to...
