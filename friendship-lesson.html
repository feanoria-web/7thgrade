What Makes a Good Friend?

🤝 Buddy

A close friend or companion

(Yakın arkadaş, kanka)

✊ Count On

To trust and rely on someone

(Güvenmek, bel bağlamak)

🛡️ Back Up

To support and defend someone

(Desteklemek, arkasında durmak)

💪 Support

To help and encourage someone

(Desteklemek, yardım etmek)

💎 Honest

Truthful and sincere

(Dürüst, samimi)

⭐ Reliable

Dependable and trustworthy

(Güvenilir)

❤️ Loyal

Faithful and devoted

(Sadık, vefalı)

📚 Key Phrases

🎯 Making Invitations

  • Would you like...?
  • How about...?
  • Let's...

✅ Accepting Invitations

  • That sounds great!
  • That sounds fun!
  • That sounds awesome!
  • I'd love to!
  • Sure!

❌ Refusing Invitations

  • I'm sorry, but I can't.
  • I'd love to, but...

💬 Giving Reasons

  • Because...
  • I must...
  • I have to...

📚 Summary & Review

What We Learned Today

Today we followed three friends' weekend story. We learned how to invite, accept, and refuse politely!

🎯 How do we invite?

  • Would you like...?
  • How about...?
  • Let's...

✅ How do we accept?

  • That sounds great/fun/awesome!
  • I'd love to!
  • Sure!
  • Yeah, that would be great!

❌ How do we refuse?

  • I'm sorry, but I can't.
  • I'd love to, but...
  • (+ Give a reason: Because... / I must... / I have to...)
